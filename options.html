Welcome. Nothing here is required to start.
Click the Page2Task icon on any page, fill in the form, and hit Add. The first time you save, Google asks you to authorize the extension: that is the only connection step, and it happens on its own.
Everything below is optional and only makes the recognition smarter.

AI recognition (optional)

Checking what AI is available…

Works without any key: free local rules detect the dates.
With an AI key, the model reads the text and writes proper task titles like “Submit Service Now ticket to cancel housing”.
Google Gemini has a free tier: get a key at aistudio.google.com (no credit card needed).

Keys are stored only on this computer: never synced to your Google account, never sent anywhere except Google's / Anthropic's official APIs. The developer cannot see them.
