Page2Task Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Page2Task is a Chrome extension that turns the page you are viewing, or a screenshot or text you paste, into a Google Tasks to-do or a Google Calendar event. It is designed so that your data goes only where you send it: to your own Google account, and to the AI option you choose. The developer stores none of your data: the only server involved is an optional relay for the free trial, described below, which keeps nothing.

What the extension accesses, and why

  • The current page (title, URL, selected text, visible text, including an open email): read only when you click the extension icon, to suggest a task title and detect deadlines. The extension cannot read pages in the background.
  • Screenshots or text you paste into the popup: used only to extract the to-do / event you asked for.
  • Your Google account (via Google's official sign-in): used only to create tasks and calendar events you explicitly ask for, to list your task lists, and to display which account is connected. Scopes requested: Google Tasks, Google Calendar events, and your email address.

Where your data goes

  • Google APIs (tasks.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com): the task or event you create is sent directly from your browser to your own Google account.
  • AI processing, chosen by you in Settings. Whichever option you pick, the request carries the content you asked it to read plus the email address of your connected Google account, so that meetings found in a conversation are titled with the other person's name rather than yours:
    • Free trial (the default for new users, first 30 reads): the text or screenshot you analyze is sent over HTTPS to our relay at page2task.sherryshen.world, which forwards it to OpenAI for one completion and returns the result. The relay keeps no logs and no copies; your Google token is used only to confirm the request comes from this extension. After 30 reads this mode stops and you choose one of the options below.
    • On-device (Chrome's built-in AI): page text, pasted text, or screenshots are processed locally on your computer. Nothing leaves your device.
    • Your own AI key (optional): if you add a Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Kimi (Moonshot) API key in Settings, the text/screenshot you analyze is sent directly from your browser to that provider under your account and their privacy terms.
    • Local rules: with neither of the above, dates are detected locally with no AI at all.
  • Nowhere else. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is stored on our side.

What is stored

  • Preferences (chosen mode, default event length, last-used task list) are stored in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.sync), which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in browsers.
  • Any API keys you enter are stored in local-only extension storage (chrome.storage.local): they stay on the computer where you typed them, are never synced, and are sent only to the AI provider they belong to.
  • The extension keeps no history of pages, emails, screenshots, or created tasks.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or for any purpose other than the single feature described above.
  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry of any kind.

Revoking access

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the extension's Settings page, and additionally revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings.
