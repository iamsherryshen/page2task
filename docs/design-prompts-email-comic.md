# Email comic prompts (Lovart)

Blast email to GSB classmates, 2026-09. Three stacked panels, one per row, before the product GIFs.
Reference style: flat line-art portrait (black wavy hair, round glasses, hoop earring, hoodie, idea bulb).
Palette is Page2Task blue, NOT the reference's yellow.

Paste the STYLE BLOCK at the top of every panel prompt. Aspect ratio 3:2 landscape (e.g. 1536x1024).
Generate panel 1 first, pick the best, then use it as the image reference for panels 2 and 3.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (paste first, every time)

Flat vector cartoon illustration, thick uniform black outlines (#1F1F1F), no shading, no gradients, no texture, solid fills only, clean minimal shapes, plenty of empty space. Strict palette: light blue background #E8F0FE, main accent blue #0B57D0, secondary pale blue #C8DDFB, deep blue #0842A0 for small accents, pure white #FFFFFF for skin and paper. The only warm color allowed is the character's small red lips. No yellow, no orange, no green.

Recurring character: a young woman with shoulder-length wavy black hair, thin round black-rimmed glasses, one small hoop earring, small red lips, wearing an oversized hoodie in blue #0B57D0 with a white drawstring. Simple face: dot eyes with a white highlight, single-line nose, short eyebrows. Same character in every panel.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no logos, no watermark. Not 3D, not photorealistic, not sketchy, not painterly.

---

## PANEL 1 — Week one of the quarter, buried

Medium shot. The character sits at a desk behind an open laptop, shoulders raised, eyes wide, one small sweat drop on her temple. The laptop screen is pouring out a flood of envelopes that spill onto the desk and pile up around her. Beside her a tall, slightly leaning stack of printed syllabus pages, twice her height, with a few sheets sliding off. Sticky notes stuck to the laptop lid and the desk edge. Blank sticky notes and envelopes, no writing on them. Composition: character slightly left of center, the paper stack on the right, envelopes filling the foreground. Overwhelmed but comic, not sad.

## PANEL 2 — Midnight, copying deadlines by hand

Medium shot, same desk, same character, now hunched forward with tired half-closed eyes and a faint frown. Left hand holds a phone showing a simple calendar grid (empty squares, no numbers); right hand holds a pen writing on a paper planner on the desk. A desk lamp on the left casts a cone of pale blue light #C8DDFB; a window behind her shows a crescent moon on the deep blue #0842A0 night sky. A coffee mug with three short curved steam lines. The laptop is still open, its screen showing a simplified email layout as blank horizontal bars. Composition: lamp left, character center, window right.

## PANEL 3 — There has to be a better way

Close-up portrait, same framing as the reference image: the character's head and shoulders, looking up and to the right with a small hopeful smile, eyebrows lifted. Above her head a single glowing lightbulb in blue #0B57D0 with short radiating dash lines around it, drawn in the same thick outline style. Background plain light blue #E8F0FE, nothing else in the frame. Calm, clean, lots of empty space. This panel leads into the product section, so keep it simple and bright.

## ALTERNATIVE — one strip in a single generation (for character consistency)

Use the STYLE BLOCK, then:

A three-panel horizontal comic strip, three equal panels side by side separated by white gutters, thin black panel borders, same character in all three panels. Panel one: the character at a desk overwhelmed, envelopes pouring out of a laptop, a tall stack of syllabus pages beside her. Panel two: late at night under a desk lamp with a crescent moon in the window, she copies deadlines from a phone into a paper planner, tired. Panel three: close-up, she looks up with a hopeful smile at a glowing blue lightbulb above her head. Widest aspect ratio available (21:9 or 3:1).

Then slice into three images. Trade-off: each panel comes out at lower resolution than a separate generation.

---

## After generating

- Pick panels whose first read is clear at 360px wide (phone). If the envelopes or papers turn to mush at that size, simplify: fewer objects, bigger shapes.
- Keep the panels free of text; captions go in the email as real HTML text.
- Send the three PNGs back; they get resized to 1200px wide (displayed at 600) and hosted on page2task.sherryshen.world.
